## What This Award Appears to Reward

Based on the application page, this award is not primarily for a future proposal. It is for:

- completed or substantially completed undergraduate research
- significant individual contribution
- clear scholarly or research impact
- strong explanation of results and applications

That means your strongest application should be centered on your completed SURCA-related research and what you personally accomplished.

## Best Positioning for Your Application

Do not frame this mainly as:

- a future summer project
- a general AI tool
- a speculative grant concept

Frame it as:

`a completed or near-completed undergraduate research study on reliability failures in LLM interpretation of special education documents, and the development of a claim-level validation approach to make those outputs more auditable`

## Category Choice

Based on your screenshots, `Engineering and Applied Sciences` is the right category.

That is the correct fit if you emphasize:

- AI reliability as an engineering problem
- document-grounded evaluation methods
- system design and validation
- real-world implications of model failure in high-stakes settings

## What Will Make the Application Strong

Your application will be strongest if it does four things clearly:

1. defines a serious real-world problem
2. shows that you used a concrete technical method
3. states what you found, not only what you plan to do
4. makes your individual contribution unmistakable

## What Reviewers Will Likely Care About

Reviewers will probably respond best to:

- the high-stakes nature of the problem
- the originality of applying claim-level validation to LLM outputs
- your personal ownership of the work
- the rigor of your method
- the broader significance beyond special education

## Most Important Framing Decision

For this award, `TRACE` should be treated as a helpful label, not the centerpiece.

A safer presentation is:

- project title focused on the research problem
- mention TRACE in the essay as the working name of the framework if needed

This keeps the application grounded in research achievement rather than branding.

## Recommended Project Title Options

### Best overall

Evaluating Claim-Level Reliability in Large Language Model Interpretation of Special Education Documents

### Slightly shorter

Claim-Level Reliability in LLM Interpretation of Special Education Documents

### More engineering-oriented

A Claim-Level Validation Framework for Reliable Interpretation of Special Education Documents

## Submission Checklist

Before submitting, make sure the application materials include:

- a clear research title
- a concise abstract centered on completed work
- a supplemental essay with methods, results, significance, and applications
- a direct paragraph on your individual contribution
- at least one sentence that broadens the significance beyond one local use case
